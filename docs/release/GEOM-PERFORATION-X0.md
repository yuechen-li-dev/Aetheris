# GEOM-PERFORATION-X0 — qualification record

**Executive verdict: Meaningful progression.** A single authored `Perforation` now builds a field of circular through openings on the `+Z` face of a Box, with deterministic layout, DFM admission, wall provenance, manifold BRep, and STEP reimport. The requested perforated constant-thickness sleeve is still unavailable.

## Implemented language and lowering

The canonical form is `Modify Panel { Perforation Vent { On: +Z; Diameter: 6mm; Layout: Hex; Pitch: 9mm; Margin: 8mm; MinimumLigament: 2mm } }`. `Layout` admits `Grid` and `Hex`. `Pitch` is horizontal center spacing; hex rows are separated by `Pitch * sqrt(3) / 2` and alternate rows shift by half a pitch. A centered lattice is the default. Optional `OffsetX` and `OffsetY` move the lattice by less than one pitch. Holes whose full rim cannot maintain the margin are omitted. The compiler emits one source feature and pattern-space IDs `Panel.Vent.RowN.ColM`, independent of BRep face numbering. Runtime correspondence maps each generated cylindrical wall to its instance and the authored Perforation source span.

The planner rejects nonpositive diameter/pitch, pitch less than or equal to diameter, insufficient `MinimumLigament`, negative margin/ligament, invalid offsets, supports too small for one opening, empty layouts, and more than 1000 openings. The materializer constructs all noninterfering analytic cylindrical voids in one bounded box composition. It checks closed manifold BRep, strict export preflight, production STEP export, and manifold STEP reimport. A shared multi-hole box builder error was corrected: straight edges now bind their full physical curve parameter interval, allowing strict preflight to validate the construction.

## Witness and measurements

`fixtures/Canonical/Perforation/flat-hex-panel.firmament` is the public flat witness. Reproduce it with:

```text
dotnet run --project Aetheris.CLI -- build fixtures/Canonical/Perforation/flat-hex-panel.firmament --output artifacts/local/perforation/flat-hex-panel.step --json
```

On the 2026-09-23 local Windows .NET 10 run, the 90 × 70 × 3 mm Box with 6 mm openings at 9 mm pitch realized **53 holes**, **220 vertices**, **171 edges**, **59 faces** and a **91,502 byte STEP** file. Native CLI wall time was **32.7 seconds**, including compilation, BRep verification, STEP export, and reimport. The SHA-256 of the STEP text was `98167F3754505DF767CEE16CEE1E2B6981CA827EEEEB5C1F04BC93454B950D3A`. A 60 × 60 × 3 mm local variant completed in **6.7 seconds** and produced a 41,635 byte STEP. A 150 × 100 × 3 mm variant spent over a minute of CPU without completing and was stopped. High-count scaling is therefore not qualified. The STEP witness is at `artifacts/local/perforation/flat-hex-panel.step`; its generated bytes remain ignored under the repository artifact policy.

The 53-hole STEP body passed strict native preflight and STEP reimport manifold checks. An OBJ display attempt did not complete within the profiling window, so triangle count and a gallery-quality render are not claimed. WASM AOT, Helios author/build/save/export, and fresh-agent UI workflows remain unqualified.

`dotnet build Aetheris.slnx --no-restore -m:1` passed with seven existing WebAssembly analyzer/toolchain warnings. The serial `dotnet test Aetheris.slnx --no-build --no-restore -m:1` passed every test project except two assertions: the generated schema list expected the old five constructs and was updated, then its targeted schema and Perforation tests passed (12/12); `SurfaceMeshIrTests.ThroughHolePlate_UsesMultiLoopPlanarBands_AndLowersToWatertightMesh` still expects 90 planar cells but gets 87. The latter uses the single-hole recipe, not the changed multi-hole builder branch, and also fails in isolation. This run does not establish an all-green kernel suite.

## Cylindrical boundary

`PipingGeometry.Pipe` already makes constant-thickness annular BRep stock from `PipePolicyIr.OuterDiameterMm` and `WallThicknessMm`. The blocker is the subsequent radial cut: `BrepBooleanSafeCompositionGraphValidator.TryValidateCylinderRootSubtract` admits only world-Z aligned cylinders on a cylinder root. The BRep curve/STEP authority has no production cylinder-to-cylinder radial intersection construction for the two curved sleeve walls and the radial opening wall. A cylindrical `Perforation` currently fails with `perforation-cylindrical-topology-unavailable` before emitting an uncut STEP file. Reusing piping stock alone cannot satisfy the sleeve request; mesh approximation or dozens of unsupported generic Boolean cuts would compromise exact topology.

## Remaining acceptance work

The cylindrical radial intersection and seam construction, high-count performance, irregular trimmed planar supports, Hollow integration, browser AOT and Helios qualification, triangle counts, and public-quality renders remain open. The static generated schema and Firmament language completion expose the planar Perforation fields and entry, and CLI build JSON reports count and stable IDs.
