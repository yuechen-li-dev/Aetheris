# Aetheris CLA questions for counsel

The business intent is recorded in [`CLA-CANDIDATE.md`](CLA-CANDIDATE.md). Please resolve these questions before the CLA becomes operative:

- Who should initially receive the license: the project owner personally, an existing or future entity, or an identified assignable successor?
- Should the contributor grant be expressly irrevocable, perpetual, and transferable, and what limits should apply?
- What exact sublicensing and relicensing wording is needed for AGPL distribution and alternative/commercial licensing?
- Should the CLA include an express patent grant, patent-defense clause, or no patent language?
- How should employer-owned work and a contributor's authority to contribute be verified without making the individual agreement burdensome?
- Is a separate corporate contributor agreement needed?
- What electronic assent or signature method is sufficient, and how should identity and agreement versions be recorded?
- How should the AGPL-continuity commitment be phrased so contributors can rely on it while allowing maintenance, successors, and independently owned components?
- Should the commitment apply to the Aetheris project as a whole, accepted Contributions, specific branches/releases, or another precisely defined scope?
- What no-warranty, limitation-of-liability, governing-law, venue, privacy, and record-retention terms are actually necessary?
- How should third-party dependencies, reference datasets, generated artifacts, and contributions incorporating separately licensed material be handled?

No repository automation should collect CLA assent until these questions are resolved and final text is approved by qualified counsel.
