# Theme design notes

## Atelier

Graphite workbench, warm/cool neutral task light, matte stock, dark high-readability edges, restrained technical grid. Preserved as the engineering default.

## Monument

Pale stone/paper gallery, warm sky and pastel fill, salmon architectural material, soft fog, quiet brown grid. Preserved as the light presentation alternative.

## Mars

Oxidized geological plate with an analytic low sun, burnt atmospheric horizon, contour-like dust strata, and deterministic sparse particulate points. A grazing orange key and ember rim create brutalist survey-equipment silhouettes; moderately rough metal keeps the part physical.

## Sirius

Deep blue-black stellar field with a blue-white analytic star, spectral halo, restrained diffraction rays, tiny deterministic stars, and one orbital trace. A hard white key, cold rim, and lower-roughness metal make CAD read as precision instrumentation without coating the UI in cyan.

## Singularity

The most severe theme: an off-axis black disk, incandescent accretion ring, logarithmic polar bands, and controlled lens rings. The focal point is placed beside the model so it cannot erase the engineering silhouette. Warm energy rim and low ambient response leave selected surfaces and edges legible. The ground grid is suppressed.

## Aeons

Deep violet observatory with enormous gold arcs, sparse star haze, diagrammatic radial spokes, and a subdued volumetric gradient. Muted gold metal and a large soft violet/gold rig make the assembly feel like an ancient machine without literal glyphs or fantasy decoration.

No additional themes were added: the four requested worlds already cover the strongest distinct directions, and a palette-swap extra would dilute the gallery.
