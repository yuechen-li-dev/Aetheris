# Aetheris Contributor License Agreement — candidate for legal review

> **Status: candidate only; pending review by qualified human legal counsel.** This text is not legal advice, is not the operative contributor agreement, and does not define a method of acceptance. Do not sign or rely on it as final terms.

This candidate records the project's business intent in short, engineer-readable form so counsel can prepare the legally operative agreement.

## 1. Contributor and contribution

The contributor identifies the person making a contribution to Aetheris and confirms that they have authority to offer that contribution under this agreement. A “Contribution” is original code, documentation, models, assets, or other material intentionally submitted for inclusion in Aetheris under the future counsel-approved acceptance process.

## 2. Ownership

The contributor retains copyright and ownership in their Contribution. This agreement is intended to grant licenses, not to assign copyright.

## 3. License grant to the project owner

The contributor grants the Aetheris project owner a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license broad enough to use, reproduce, publicly display, publicly perform, modify, prepare derivative works of, distribute, sublicense, and license the Contribution as part of Aetheris:

- under GNU Affero General Public License v3.0; and
- under alternative or commercial licenses offered alongside the AGPL version.

Counsel must determine the exact operative wording, duration, revocability, sublicensing language, recipient, successor treatment, and whether any patent terms are appropriate.

## 4. Continuing AGPL availability

The intended exchange is: the contributor keeps ownership and permits AGPL and alternative licensing; the project owner commits that Aetheris will continue to be made available under GNU AGPL-3.0. Alternative licenses may coexist with the AGPL version but are not intended to replace it or turn the community version into a proprietary-only product.

Counsel must refine this commitment so contributors can understand and rely on its legally operative scope without accidentally preventing ordinary project evolution.

## 5. Right to contribute and restrictions

The contributor represents that they have the right to submit the Contribution and grant the required rights. The contributor will disclose known third-party licenses, employer ownership, confidentiality obligations, or other restrictions that may affect its use. The contributor does not knowingly submit material they are not authorized to contribute.

## 6. Warranty treatment

The project expects a conventional contribution “as is” and no-warranty provision, but counsel must decide whether and how to include it. No warranty language in this candidate should be treated as approved.

## 7. Review and acceptance

This candidate deliberately omits governing law, venue, arbitration, electronic-signature mechanics, corporate-contributor machinery, patent strategy, and employer-specific conclusions. It becomes operative only after human legal review, project-owner approval, publication of final text, and publication of an unambiguous acceptance process.
