# Composition history

`SafeBooleanComposition` remains above Recipes as recognized-family
construction history, not generic BRep metadata. The through-hole request
retains the exact history containing root kind, feature identity, analytic
descriptor, axis/span/radii, and continuation state; its result preserves that
same object. Polygonal facade rebuilding continues to attach its admitted
through-void history after recipe realization.

Stepped/counterbore, conic, mixed-family, and continuation policies remain in
their historical classifiers/builders. M4 does not erase or generalize them.
