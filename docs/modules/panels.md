# Panels

## Surface construction versus engineering object

A mathematical surface answers “where is the support and how was it constructed?” A `PanelIr` answers “what bounded, oriented engineering object is being assembled?” These are deliberately different values:

```text
ParametricSurface / RuledSurface / SectionSurface / BoundaryPatch
    -> bounded Panel
    -> ordered semantic PanelEdge values
    -> Interface / Mate relations
    -> Panel network
```

A Panel is neither a naked support nor necessarily a closed solid Part. M0 is BRep-backed, rectangular-domain/four-sided, and retains the source construction so a later Panel implementation may use BRep, SubD, or SDF backing without changing the high-level edge and Mate contract. Arbitrary trim networks and SubD/SDF construction are not implemented.

## M0 model

`PanelIr` owns a stable ID, `PanelConstruction`, rectangular parameter domain, four explicitly ordered `PanelEdgeIr` members, four corners, normal/material-side orientation, optional positive thickness metadata, optional material text, developability evidence, approximation certificate, and construction provenance. Thickness is engineering metadata; it does not turn the Panel into a solid.

Front orientation follows `dP/du × dP/dv`. Its closed boundary winding is `South`, `East`, `North`, `West`: South follows increasing u, East increasing v, North decreasing u, and West decreasing v. Back orientation reverses both the support normal and loop traversal. Author-facing compass names remain stable while `SourceDirection` records whether an edge follows or opposes its source parameter.

Corners are stable semantic points named `SW`, `SE`, `NE`, and `NW`. Panel and member IDs are deterministic, for example `panel:Canopy`, `panel:Canopy:edge:east`, and `panel:Canopy:corner:ne`.

## Semantic edges and Mates

Each boundary edge is a `SemanticValue` with `CurveCapable`, `BoundaryEdgeCapable`, `ExactGeometryCapable`, and `Selectable`, backed by `ExactCurveBinding`. The binding contains a directed source curve and parameter interval. Raw BRep `EdgeId` is not part of the authoring contract.

The existing Assembly `InterfaceDefinition`/`MateIr` path handles Panel seams. A two-role Interface requiring `BoundaryEdgeCapable` validates its exact curve bindings. `Correspondence: OppositeDirections` is the default physical seam mapping; `SameDirection` is explicit. M0 measures both paired endpoints and 17 deterministic curve samples. It reports endpoint and G0 residuals, duplicate one-to-one edge use, free edges, and missing exact bindings. `Continuity: G1` binds cleanly but produces `assembly-panel-mate-g1-unsupported`; M0 never claims tangent continuity it cannot verify.

Mating relates separate Panels. It neither Boolean-unions them nor implies a closed shell.

## Developability and fabrication seam

Developability evidence is copied from the construction onto the Panel as `Developable`, `NonDevelopable`, or `Indeterminate`. A ruled Panel is not automatically developable: the current ruled classifier retains its sampled normalized scalar-triple-product evidence. Section, boundary, and general parametric Panels remain indeterminate where no bounded classifier exists.

The intended future lowering is:

```text
Developable Panel + thickness + material
    -> SheetMetal panel
    -> flat pattern
```

That consumer must reject `NonDevelopable` and decide explicitly what to do with `Indeterminate`. Panel M0 does not flatten, infer neutral axes, or materialize thickness as the authoritative object.

## BRep, STEP, mesh, and drawing status

The Panel's support and directed boundaries are BRep-compatible. The current AP242 exporter only writes closed `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`, so evidence export uses the existing thin closed export envelope while Firmament/PanelIR remains semantic authority. STEP does not preserve Aetheris Panel member names.

`SurfaceMeshIR` still rejects non-rational B-spline support surfaces with typed unsupported coverage; Panel M0 does not hide or solve that limitation. Existing legacy display tessellation and HLR may consume admitted BReps, but no Panel-specific Drawing contract is claimed.

Evidence is generated by `aetheris modules showcase --out-dir docs/modules/artifacts/panel-m0` and includes canonical Firmament, compiler proof, edge tables, network/Mate residuals, performance, determinism, and STEP files.
