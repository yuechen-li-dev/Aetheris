The front source contour is planar but not a circle: the best circle fit has radial RMS 1.984 mm and max 3.228 mm; the fitted ellipse improves radial RMS to 1.218 mm and max 1.815 mm. The final cubic approximation is non-rational. Its 0.1° seam is roughly 0.14 mm wide at the front section and 0.047 mm at the rear section. The cut planes differ substantially; a coaxial revolve would put the front rim in the wrong plane. The previous hand-authored chain paired a rear span starting at local +X with a front span starting near world +Y, producing the visible twist. `CommonRay` now pairs both starts along world +Y. A trial using exact clockwise circular arcs failed SectionChain's pcurve inverse check by about 0.0004–0.0015 mm against its 0.00001 mm limit. The bounded loft's cubic spans materialize with 60 pcurves and maximum reconstruction deviation about 5.5×10⁻¹⁰ mm.

`aetheris asm inspect` reports no diagnostics. `aetheris asm export-ap242` produced `artifacts/local/lamp-visible-intent.step` (81,118 bytes, SHA-256 `1388CA985A1002E80A356F086B238A63FC9CD03FD7ED4C1755398BD7E9FCDCEF`). A repeat export produced the same hash. `aetheris asm import-step` succeeded. The product has one assembly product plus seven part products, seven `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`/`CLOSED_SHELL` pairs, 43 advanced faces, 21 planes, 11 cylinders, and 9 non-rational B-spline surfaces. `RATIONAL_B_SPLINE_CURVE` and `RATIONAL_B_SPLINE_SURFACE` entity counts are both zero; no rational pcurve entity is present. The SectionChain's self-intersection and pcurve checks pass. The 63° twist witness also STEP-reimports, with 60 valid pcurves and measured mid-height radius `25 cos(31.5°)` mm. The added plain `Loft` circle-to-ellipse witness STEP-reimports as a closed six-face solid with 24 valid pcurves.

Three local variants under ignored `artifacts/local/lamp-edit-checks/` passed `aetheris asm inspect` with zero diagnostics: opening radius 61.6→60 mm, pole height 429→439 mm with dependent pivot/head/shade stations raised 10 mm, and front ellipse axis lengths increased by 5%. The pole edit currently requires coordinated station edits; the assembly does not yet own a mate that drives those placements. The shade width is now one editable `Ellipse2` declaration.

The front and isometric overlays compare selected source BRep edges (gray) with replacement STEP-backed assembly edges (cyan). They are visual review aids, not a full surface-distance proof. Source objects 13–14 (connector/cable), 25–26 (internal electrical details), bulb internals, and the underside base pocket are the explicit exclusion mask. The simple nut is retained but moved below the flat base; this displacement is **not** excluded from the component comparison. A whole-object nearest sampled-edge comparison is not a valid surface score here because source objects contain many interior/pocket edges with no intended counterpart. Full per-component surface RMS/p95/max therefore remain unqualified rather than replaced with misleading edge-cloud numbers.

## Automation and judgment

The local 3DM inspection/recovery tooling mechanically identified BRep objects, units, cylindrical axes, radii, section planes, edge samples, and envelopes. The bounded fit and overlay scripts measured the shade contour and regenerated source-to-model edge views. The production CLI supplies materialization, STEP export/reimport, topology, and edit diagnostics. The agent judgment was the component decomposition, the omission mask, choosing a tilted ruled shade instead of a revolve, selecting nominal wall/shaft sizes, and retaining the underside nut as separate hardware. An independent fresh agent given the source inventory and public Firmament docs proposed the same primitive/ruled component decomposition and excluded wire and the underside pocket.

The next bounded capability is an annular SectionChain section or another exact thin-wall construction that closes the shade without a slit while retaining oblique front and rear frames. Qualify that change with exact STEP reimport and component surface residuals; avoid reconstructing the Rhino NURBS control net.
